Prithvi row in `docs/SOURCES.md`, which did not discover the official JSON
schedule used by Prithvi's own booking page.

Discovery leads may come from search, forums, ticketing sites, and local media,
but publication authority must be the venue or organiser. Aggregators are not
accepted as the factual source.

## Recommended next source

Royal Opera House is the next clean Mumbai lane after Prithvi. Its seven
performances already clear the product-value bar, use one static official page,
have stable first-party detail suffixes, and do not inherit the NCPA or NMACC
policy blockers. Build it only after Prithvi passes the Bright and backend city
gates; adding two sources at once would make failures harder to attribute.
